Wristnote 使用帮助

隐私政策 · English version below

Wristnote 让你在 Apple Watch 上一键录下会议、课堂和访谈，录音自动传到 iPhone，在本机转成文字，再用你自己的 AI 服务整理成笔记。需要 iPhone（iOS 18 或更新）和配对的 Apple Watch（watchOS 11 或更新）。

在手表上怎么录

  • 点一下 C 开始录音；录音中点右下角的 M 珠子，给这一刻打个标记。
  • 点圆环暂停或继续；按住圆环片刻结束。
  • 录音会自动传到 iPhone。打开 iPhone 上的 Wristnote 会传得更快；也可以在 我的 → 我的手表 点“立即同步”。
  • 没带手表时，点 iPhone 底部中间的按钮就能直接在手机上录音；也可以在录音库点“导入音频”。

转写

  • 录音在 iPhone 上离线转成文字，不花钱、不上传。
  • iOS 26 的识别效果最好。iOS 18–25 使用系统的离线听写：第一次使用某种语言前，请在 设置 → 通用 → 键盘 打开“启用听写”，让系统下载该语言。
  • iOS 18–25 对较远或较轻的声音不太敏感，录音时请让手表靠近说话的人。转不出文字时，可以在录音页点“重新本机转写”。
  • 录的是外语：转写语言为中文、日语或韩语时，如果结果几乎全是拉丁字母，会自动改用英文再转一次。也可以在录音页点“换语言重新转写”，选另一种语言。

说话人

  • 转写后会在本机自动区分说话人，原文换人处显示彩色名字。2–4 人轮流发言时效果最好。
  • 点任意一个名字，可以一次给所有人填名字；几个声音填同一个名字，就会合并成一个人。

专业术语

  • 在 我的 → 专业术语库 勾选行业词库（科技、商业、医疗、法律、教育、营销、工程、设计，可多选），也可以添加自己的人名、产品名和缩写。
  • AI 整理原文、笔记、问答和翻译会参考这些写法。iOS 26 的本机识别不使用术语表，专业名词主要靠 AI 整理纠正。

AI 整理、笔记、问答与翻译

  • 这些功能使用你自己的 API Key：在 我的 → AI 服务 选择 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、DeepSeek 或兼容 OpenAI 的自定义服务，填入 Key，点“测试连接”。
  • 费用由你选的服务商直接向你收取，整理一段录音通常只需几分钱。我们不经手你的 Key，也不收取任何费用。
  • 第一次保存 Key 时会请你确认是否同意把文字发送给该服务商，可随时在 我的 → AI 服务 撤回。只会发送文字（转写文字、术语和说话人名字），不发送音频。不想自动整理或自动生成笔记，可在 我的 → 我的手表 关闭。

录音卡在“传输中断”

手表没电、手表上的 App 被删除或重装时，一段录音可能只传了一部分。超过 30 分钟没有新数据，录音库顶部会显示“传输中断”。点开后可以“保存已收到的部分”（缺的部分会在原文里标出），或者删除。

通知

Wristnote 会在笔记已生成、手表录音没有传完、AI 服务出错、手表录音被中断时发本机通知。每一类都可以在 我的 → 通知 单独关闭；如果系统里关掉了通知，那一页会提示你去系统设置打开。

数据与备份

录音和文字只保存在你的 iPhone 上，会随 iPhone 备份（包括 iCloud 备份）。没有账号，也没有云端同步。详见隐私政策。

Wristnote Support

Privacy Policy

Wristnote lets you record meetings, classes and interviews from your Apple Watch with one tap. Recordings move to your iPhone, are transcribed on the device, and are turned into notes by the AI service you choose. Requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a paired Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.

Recording on the watch

  • Tap the C to start. While recording, tap the M bead at the lower right to mark the moment.
  • Tap the ring to pause or resume; press and hold the ring to finish.
  • Recordings transfer to your iPhone automatically. Opening Wristnote on the iPhone makes it faster, or tap Sync now in Account → My watch.
  • No watch with you? Tap the center button at the bottom of the iPhone app to record on the phone, or tap Import in the Library.

Transcription

  • Recordings are transcribed offline on your iPhone: free and never uploaded.
  • iOS 26 gives the best results. iOS 18–25 uses the system's offline dictation: before using a language for the first time, turn on Enable Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard so the system downloads it.
  • On iOS 18–25, quiet or distant speech is harder to recognize, so keep the watch close to the speaker. If no text comes out, tap Re-transcribe on-device in the recording.
  • Recorded in another language? If the transcription language is Chinese, Japanese or Korean and the result is almost all Latin letters, Wristnote automatically tries again in English. You can also tap Re-transcribe in Another Language in the recording.

Speakers

  • After transcription, speakers are told apart on the device and each change of speaker shows a colored name. Works best with 2–4 people taking turns.
  • Tap any name to name everyone at once; give several voices the same name to merge them into one person.

Vocabulary

  • In Account → Vocabulary, select industry vocabularies (tech, business, healthcare, legal, education, marketing, engineering, design; as many as you like) and add your own names, product names and abbreviations.
  • AI clean-up, notes, Q&A and translation follow these spellings. On iOS 26, on-device recognition does not use the vocabulary, so jargon is mainly corrected by AI clean-up.

AI clean-up, notes, Q&A and translation

  • These features use your own API key: in Account → AI service, choose OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek or a custom OpenAI-compatible service, enter your key and tap Test connection.
  • Your provider bills you directly; cleaning up one recording usually costs a few cents. We never see your key and charge nothing.
  • When you first save a key, you are asked to agree to sending text to that provider; you can withdraw in Account → AI service. Only text is sent (transcript, vocabulary terms and speaker names), never audio. To stop automatic clean-up or automatic notes, switch them off in Account → My watch.

A recording shows “Transfer interrupted”

If the watch runs out of battery, or the watch app is deleted or reinstalled, a recording may arrive only in part. After 30 minutes without new data, the top of the Library shows “Transfer interrupted”. Open it to save what arrived (the missing part is marked in the transcript) or to delete it.

Notifications

Wristnote sends local notifications when notes are ready, when a watch recording didn't finish transferring, when your AI service reports an error, and when a watch recording is interrupted. Turn each one off in Account → Notifications; if notifications are off in system Settings, that page shows how to turn them back on.

Data and backup

Recordings and text stay on your iPhone and are included in iPhone backups (including iCloud Backup). There is no account and no cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy.
