Wristnote 使用帮助

隐私政策 · English version below

Wristnote 让你在 Apple Watch 上一键录下会议、课堂和访谈，录音自动传到 iPhone，在本机转成文字，再用你自己的 AI 服务整理成笔记。需要 iPhone（iOS 18 或更新）和配对的 Apple Watch（watchOS 11 或更新）。

在手表上怎么录

  • 点一下 C 开始录音；录音中点右下角的 M 珠子，给这一刻打个标记。
  • 点圆环暂停或继续；按住圆环片刻结束。
  • 录音会自动传到 iPhone。打开 iPhone 上的 Wristnote 会传得更快。

转写

  • 录音在 iPhone 上离线转成文字，不花钱、不上传。
  • iOS 26 的识别效果最好。iOS 18–25 使用系统的离线听写：第一次使用某种语言前，请在 设置 → 通用 → 键盘 打开“启用听写”，让系统下载该语言。
  • iOS 18–25 对较远或较轻的声音不太敏感，录音时请让手表靠近说话的人。转不出文字时，可以在录音页点“重新本机转写”。

AI 整理、笔记、问答与翻译

  • 这些功能使用你自己的 API Key：在 我的 → AI 服务 选择 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、DeepSeek 或兼容 OpenAI 的自定义服务，填入 Key，点“测试连接”。
  • 费用由你选的服务商直接向你收取，整理一段录音通常只需几分钱。我们不经手你的 Key，也不收取任何费用。
  • 只会发送文字，不发送音频。不想自动整理或自动生成笔记，可在 我的 → 我的手表 关闭。

录音卡在“传输中断”

手表没电、手表上的 App 被删除或重装时，一段录音可能只传了一部分。超过 30 分钟没有新数据，录音库顶部会显示“传输中断”。点开后可以“保存已收到的部分”（缺的部分会在原文里标出），或者删除。

数据与备份

录音和文字只保存在你的 iPhone 上，会随 iPhone 备份（包括 iCloud 备份）。没有账号，也没有云端同步。详见隐私政策。

Wristnote Support

Privacy Policy

Wristnote lets you record meetings, classes and interviews from your Apple Watch with one tap. Recordings move to your iPhone, are transcribed on the device, and are turned into notes by the AI service you choose. Requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a paired Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.

Recording on the watch

  • Tap the C to start. While recording, tap the M bead at the lower right to mark the moment.
  • Tap the ring to pause or resume; press and hold the ring to finish.
  • Recordings transfer to your iPhone automatically. Opening Wristnote on the iPhone makes it faster.

Transcription

  • Recordings are transcribed offline on your iPhone: free and never uploaded.
  • iOS 26 gives the best results. iOS 18–25 uses the system's offline dictation: before using a language for the first time, turn on Enable Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard so the system downloads it.
  • On iOS 18–25, quiet or distant speech is harder to recognize, so keep the watch close to the speaker. If no text comes out, tap Re-transcribe on-device in the recording.

AI clean-up, notes, Q&A and translation

  • These features use your own API key: in Account → AI service, choose OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek or a custom OpenAI-compatible service, enter your key and tap Test connection.
  • Your provider bills you directly; cleaning up one recording usually costs a few cents. We never see your key and charge nothing.
  • Only text is sent, never audio. To stop automatic clean-up or automatic notes, switch them off in Account → My watch.

A recording shows “Transfer interrupted”

If the watch runs out of battery, or the watch app is deleted or reinstalled, a recording may arrive only in part. After 30 minutes without new data, the top of the Library shows “Transfer interrupted”. Open it to save what arrived (the missing part is marked in the transcript) or to delete it.

Data and backup

Recordings and text stay on your iPhone and are included in iPhone backups (including iCloud Backup). There is no account and no cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy.
