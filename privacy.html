Wristnote 隐私政策

使用帮助 · 买断版 · 最后更新：2026-10-17 · English version below

一句话

我们不收集你的任何数据。Wristnote 没有账号、没有登录、没有统计分析，也没有接收你内容的服务器。

录音与文字保存在哪里

  • 手表录音传到你的 iPhone。录音、转写文字、笔记、搜索与播放都在 iPhone 本机完成。
  • 转写在 iPhone 上由 Apple 的系统语音模型离线完成，音频不会因此离开设备。每种语言首次使用时系统可能需要联网下载一次语音模型。
  • 区分说话人也在 iPhone 本机完成，使用随 App 打包的模型，不联网；音频和声音特征都不会离开设备。你给说话人起的名字只保存在本机资料库里。
  • 资料库保存在 App 的本机存储中，没有被排除在备份之外，因此会按你的 iOS 设置包含在 iPhone 备份（包括 iCloud 备份或电脑备份）里，由 Apple 或你自己的电脑保管，我们无法访问。
  • 你可以随时在 App 内删除录音；删除 App 会删除本机数据。

AI 功能与你自选的服务商

  • 翻译、总结、整理原文、润色和 AI 问答是可选功能。没有填入 API Key 时，任何内容都不会被发送。第一次保存 Key（或更换服务商、服务地址）时，App 会弹窗说明会发送哪些内容、发给哪个服务商，只有你点“同意”之后才会发送；你可以随时在 我的 → AI 服务 撤回同意，撤回后不再发送。同意之后：每次本机转写完成，「整理原文」和「自动生成笔记」会自动运行（默认都开启），两者都可以在 我的 → 我的手表 里关闭；其他 AI 功能在你使用时才发送。相关文字会从你的 iPhone 直接发送给你选择的服务商（OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、DeepSeek，或你填写的兼容 OpenAI 的自定义服务）。
  • 发送的只有完成该功能所需的文字：转写文字，以及你的专业术语（所选的行业词库和自定义术语）和你给说话人起的名字。绝不发送音频。
  • 这些文字由该服务商按其自己的条款和隐私政策处理，我们从不接收这些文字，它们直接发往你选择的服务商；若你填写的是自定义（兼容 OpenAI）地址，则由运营该地址的一方按其条款处理。请在使用前阅读所选服务商的政策。
  • API Key 只保存在这台设备的钥匙串（Keychain）里，不会同步或迁移到其他设备；不会上传给我们，也不会写入日志或导出文件。

实时听写与实时翻译

「工具」里的实时听写和实时翻译使用 Apple 的系统语音识别，并未强制仅在本机处理，Apple 可能在其服务器上处理你说话的音频，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。只有你主动开启时才会使用麦克风，并且只保存识别出的文字，不保存音轨。

Apple Watch

手表只负责录音并通过系统蓝牙/WatchConnectivity 传给配对的 iPhone，不会把录音发送到其他地方。

通知

Wristnote 只使用本机通知（笔记已生成、手表录音没有传完、AI 服务出错、手表录音被中断），由 iPhone 或手表在本机生成。没有推送服务器，不会上传设备标识，也不因此收集任何数据。你可以在 我的 → 通知 或系统设置里关闭。

权限

麦克风：录音与实时听写。语音识别：把录音转为文字。文件访问：仅在你主动导入或导出时使用。通知：仅用于上述本机提醒。

儿童

Wristnote 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

若将来发生变化，我们会更新本页并修改上方日期。

Wristnote Privacy Policy

Support · Paid edition · Last updated: 2026-10-17

In short

We collect no data about you. Wristnote has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no server that receives your content.

Where your recordings and text live

  • Recordings from Apple Watch are transferred to your iPhone. Recording, transcription, notes, search and playback all happen on the iPhone.
  • Transcription of recordings runs offline on the iPhone using Apple's on-device speech models, so audio does not leave the device. The system may need to download a language's speech model once, the first time you use it.
  • Speaker identification also runs on the iPhone, using a model bundled with the app and no network connection; neither the audio nor any voice characteristics leave the device. Speaker names you enter are stored only in the local library.
  • The library is stored in the app's local storage and is not excluded from backups. Depending on your iOS settings it is included in iPhone backups (iCloud or computer backups), which are held by Apple or by you; we cannot access them.
  • You can delete recordings in the app at any time; deleting the app removes the local data.

AI features and the provider you choose

  • Translation, summaries, polishing, cleaned-up transcripts and AI Q&A are optional. Without an API key, nothing is ever sent. When you first save a key (or switch to another provider or address), the app shows what will be sent and to which provider, and nothing is sent until you tap Agree. You can withdraw consent at any time in Account → AI service; after that nothing is sent. Once you have agreed, transcript clean-up and automatic notes run after each on-device transcription (both on by default); you can switch either off in Account → My watch. Other AI features send text when you use them. The relevant text is sent directly from your iPhone to the provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or a custom OpenAI-compatible service you enter).
  • Only the text needed for that feature is sent: the transcript text, plus your vocabulary terms (the industry vocabularies you selected and your own terms) and the speaker names you entered. Audio is never sent.
  • That provider processes the text under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive this text; it goes directly to the service you choose. For a custom (OpenAI-compatible) address, that is whoever runs that address, under their terms. Please read your provider's policy before use.
  • Your API key is stored only in this device's Keychain; it is not synced or migrated to other devices. It is never uploaded to us, logged, or included in exports.

Live dictation and live translation

Live dictation and live translation in Tools use Apple's system speech recognition and are not forced to run on-device. Apple may process the audio of your speech on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy. The microphone is used only when you start it, and only the recognized text is saved, not the audio.

Apple Watch

The watch only records and sends audio to your paired iPhone through the system. It does not send recordings anywhere else.

Notifications

Wristnote uses local notifications only (notes ready, watch transfer not finished, AI service error, watch recording interrupted), created on your iPhone or watch. There is no push server, no device token is uploaded, and no data is collected. You can turn them off in Account → Notifications or in system Settings.

Permissions

Microphone: recording and live dictation. Speech recognition: turning recordings into text. File access: only when you import or export. Notifications: only for the local alerts above.

Children

Wristnote is not directed to children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone.
